[Ask a teammate] Call this when, while working, you are missing a value only a teammate of the same company knows (a colleague's contact number, date of birth or car plate, a schedule, a figure, who is in charge, an internal decision …). Never invent the value or just leave a blank and stop: keep the blank marker (such as [to confirm]) in the file and the reply draft as it is, and use this tool to ask the person whether to ask the teammate who would know. This tool sends nothing to anyone — it only puts a card "Ask … on the messenger?" in the chat; the request goes to that teammate's one-to-one messenger room only when the person allows it. When the answer comes, the system fills the blanks. Never send any mail.
- member: the teammate's name (as the mail or file says it; when there is only a title, find the name) or e-mail. When they are not on the team list the tool returns the list: pick from it. Never for people outside the company (customers, other companies).
- reason: one sentence on why it is needed (which mail or work needs what). No mail text and no one else's personal data.
- fields: the items to fill. A short label ("Phone"), "Dana Park phone" when they are for several people. When you left a blank in a file, put the line holding it, character for character, in line, and the blank marker inside that line (e.g. [to confirm]) in blank.
- file: the name of the draft file with the blanks (the file you made in this turn).
After calling it, say only briefly what you left blank and that a card asks whether to ask them.
