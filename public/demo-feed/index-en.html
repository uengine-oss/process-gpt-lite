Crewon AI — talk recap2026 AI × RMF Workshop · Korea Institute of Information Security & Cryptology

At the 2026 AI × RMF Workshop hosted by the Korea Institute of Information Security & Cryptology, we presented what security AI agents can do, under the title Running NIST RMF with ontology-based Semantic AI Agents.

The talk introduced Crewon AI's technical architecture and shared the concept and use scenarios of the RMF Coordinator AI Crew, designed so that AI agents can support and carry out the whole NIST RMF (Risk Management Framework) process.

Cyber attacks are getting smarter as they combine with AI, and they now move faster than people can analyse and respond to one by one. To answer AI-driven attacks, defence has to become AI-centred too.

To meet this change, Crewon AI is building an ontology-based Semantic AI Agent Runtime: a platform that links a company's systems, data, organisation, owners, business processes, rules and policies in an ontology, so AI agents understand the business context and can plan, reason and do real work on their own.

The RMF Coordinator AI Crew links the seven NIST RMF steps — Prepare, Categorize, Select, Implement, Assess, Authorize, Monitor — into one workflow and coordinates the specialist AI crew of each step with people and security systems. Events detected by existing SIEM, EDR and security appliances are tied to the company's assets, business, security controls and approval history, interpreted semantically, assessed for risk; the needed actions are planned and carried out, and the results are verified again and kept as evidence.

Add crewai.work's ITSM, e-approval, task management and collaboration, and you get a new operating model where AI and people do security work together. Of course, people remain responsible for important decisions and for accepting risk. Every AI action is governed by policy and permissions, and the process has to be traceable and auditable.

"AI goes from tool to teammate." — we want to build the AI-Native Workplace.
